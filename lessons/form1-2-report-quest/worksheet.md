# Report Quest — Form 1 & 2 Worksheet

**Subject:** English (KSSM, CEFR A1–A2) · **Level:** Form 1 & 2 · **Skill:** Writing a short report (50–80 words)

Name: ______________________  Class: ________  Team: ______________  Coins: ____

> **Remember the Report Burger:** TITLE → START (When? Where? Who?) → MIDDLE (What happened? First, Then, Finally) → END (How was it? Next time, we should…)
> A report tells **what happened**, so use the **past tense** (go → went).

## Boss Level: Write Your Report (10 coins)

Your class celebrated Teachers’ Day. Write a report in **50–80 words**. Use the notes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **WHO** | Form 2 students |
| **WHEN** | 16 May |
| **WHERE** | school hall |
| **WHAT** | sang a song, gave cards, watched a sketch |
| **HOW** | happy, teachers smiled |

**Helper frame** (you can use it):

> **Teachers’ Day**
> On ________, ________________ celebrated Teachers’ Day in the ____________.
> First, we ____________________. Then, we ____________________.
> Finally, we ____________________.
> It was ____________. Next time, we should ____________________.

**My checklist**

- [ ] I wrote a title.
- [ ] START: I wrote when, where and who.
- [ ] MIDDLE: I used First, Then and Finally.
- [ ] END: I wrote how it was and an idea for next time.
- [ ] I used past tense verbs.

---

## Sample answers (for discussion in class)

**Sample A: Good (about 50 words, uses the helper frame)**

> **Teachers’ Day**
> On 16 May, Form 2 students celebrated Teachers’ Day in the school hall. First, we sang a song. Then, we gave cards to our teachers. Finally, we watched a sketch. It was a happy day. Next time, we should make a cake for our teachers.

**Sample B: Even better (about 75 words, adds more details)**

> **Teachers’ Day 2026**
> On 16 May, Form 2 students celebrated Teachers’ Day in the school hall. First, we sang a special song for our teachers. Then, we gave them colourful cards. Some students also gave flowers. After that, we watched a funny sketch about school life. Everyone laughed! Finally, we took photos together. It was a happy day because our teachers smiled a lot. Next time, we should make a video for them.

**Talk about it**

1. Find the four parts (Title, Start, Middle, End) in Sample A.
2. Circle all the past tense verbs in Sample B.
3. What extra details did Sample B add? (colourful cards, flowers, a funny sketch, photos, because…)
4. Which new words can you use in your own report? (*After that, Everyone, because*)
