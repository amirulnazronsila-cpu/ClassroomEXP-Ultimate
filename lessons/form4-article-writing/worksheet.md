## Answer key (teacher)

**Level 1:** informs, entertains or persuades · many readers (magazine, newsletter, blog) · a catchy title + “by [name]” · semi-formal, lively and personal · “you”, questions, linkers, idioms · a final thought or call to action

**Level 2:** 1 R · 2 A · 3 R · 4 A · 5 A · 6 R

**Level 4:** C. It is catchy and asks the reader a question. A is too short and dull; B sounds like a report title.

**Boss Battle: sample answer (about 220 words)**

> **Stay Healthy, Stay Happy**
> *by Daniel Lee, 4 Cemerlang*
>
> Do you often feel tired, moody or unable to focus in class? If so, your body might be asking for a little more care. The good news is that staying healthy is easier than you think.
>
> Firstly, eat a balanced diet. Instead of skipping breakfast or filling up on fried snacks from the canteen, try to include fruit, vegetables and protein in your meals. Your brain needs good fuel to work well.
>
> Moreover, exercise regularly. You do not need to run a marathon! A thirty-minute walk, a game of badminton with friends or even dancing in your room can boost your energy and lift your mood.
>
> In addition, get enough sleep. Many of us stay up late scrolling through social media, but teenagers need about eight to ten hours of sleep each night. A well-rested mind remembers more and stresses less.
>
> Finally, take care of your mental health. Talk to a friend, a family member or the school counsellor when you feel worried. Sharing your problems is a sign of strength, not weakness.
>
> In conclusion, a healthy body and a happy mind go hand in hand. So, why not choose one tip and start today? Your future self will thank you!
