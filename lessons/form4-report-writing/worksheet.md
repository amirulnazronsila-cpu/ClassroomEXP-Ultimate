# Report Writing Made Easy — Form 4 Worksheet

**Subject:** English (KSSM, CEFR-aligned) · **Level:** Form 4 · **Exam link:** SPM Paper 2 (Writing) — a report can appear in Part 2 (125–150 words) or Part 3 (200–250 words)

---

## Exercise 1: Sort it out

Write the section each sentence belongs to: **I** (Introduction), **F** (Findings), **R** (Recommendation) or **C** (Conclusion).

| | Sentence | Section |
|---|---|---|
| A | It is recommended that more bins be placed at the canteen. | ____ |
| B | The purpose of this report is to describe the Recycling Week. | ____ |
| C | A total of 450 kg of paper was collected by students. | ____ |
| D | In conclusion, the campaign should be held every year. | ____ |
| E | However, many plastic bottles were not rinsed properly. | ____ |
| F | A poster competition was also organised by the Green Club. | ____ |

**Bonus:** Write the letters in the correct report order: ___ → ___ → ___ → ___ → ___ → ___

---

## Exercise 2: Your turn (SPM Part 2 style)

You are the Secretary of the Environmental Club. Your school held a Gotong-royong Day last Saturday. The principal, Encik Ahmad, has asked you to write a report.

Use **all** the notes below. Write **125–150 words**. Time: 25 minutes.

- Date and place: 18 October, school compound
- 200 students and 15 teachers took part
- Cleaned drains, planted 50 trees, painted the fence
- Problem: not enough gloves and brooms
- Your own recommendation

### Peer checklist

- [ ] To / From / Date / Subject
- [ ] Purpose stated in the introduction
- [ ] Subheadings, and every note expanded
- [ ] Formal language, past tense, no slang or contractions
- [ ] A recommendation, and the word count is within 125–150

---

## Answer key (teacher)

**Exercise 1:** A – R · B – I · C – F · D – C · E – F · F – F
**Bonus order:** B → C → F → E → A → D

**Exercise 2: sample answer (about 140 words)**

> **To:** Encik Ahmad, Principal
> **From:** Nur Aisyah binti Rahman, Secretary, Environmental Club
> **Date:** 22 October 2026
> **Subject:** Report on the Gotong-royong Day
>
> **Introduction**
> The purpose of this report is to inform you about the Gotong-royong Day, which was held in the school compound on 18 October.
>
> **Participants and activities**
> A total of 200 students and 15 teachers took part. The drains around the school were cleaned, and 50 trees were planted near the field. In addition, the fence at the main gate was painted.
>
> **Problems**
> However, there were not enough gloves and brooms, so some students had to wait for their turn.
>
> **Recommendations**
> It is recommended that the club request a sponsorship from the PIBG to buy more tools. The event should also be held every term.
>
> **Conclusion**
> Overall, the event was a success and made the school cleaner and greener.
>
> Prepared by,
> Nur Aisyah binti Rahman
