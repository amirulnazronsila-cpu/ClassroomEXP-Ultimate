# Article Arena — Form 1 & 2 Worksheet

**Subject:** English (KSSM, CEFR A1–A2) · **Level:** Form 1 & 2 · **Skill:** Writing a short article (50–80 words)

Name: ______________________  Class: ________  Team: ______________  XP: ____

> **REPORT** tells **what happened** to a teacher or principal (past tense: *went, had*).
> **ARTICLE** gives **ideas and tips** to **many readers** and talks to **you**.
> **Article parts:** TITLE → HELLO! (ask a question) → TIPS (First, Also, Finally) → BYE! (give advice)

---

## Quest 1: Report or Article? (+10 XP each)

Write **R** (report) or **A** (article).

1. “Last Friday, our class went to the zoo.” ____
2. “Do you want strong teeth? Read on!” ____
3. “You should sleep early every night.” ____
4. “First, the teachers gave a talk.” ____
5. “3 Easy Ways to Save Water” ____
6. “It was a fun day. Next time, we should bring hats.” ____

---

## Quest 2: Transform! (+10 XP each)

Change the report sentence into an article sentence. Use a **question**, **you** or **advice**.

| Report | Article |
|---|---|
| We cycled around the school field. | ______________________________ |
| The students ate fruit at recess. | ______________________________ |
| Our class cleaned the beach. | ______________________________ |

---

## Quest 3: Fill the Gaps (+10 XP each)

**Loot box:** Do · First · Also · You · should

**Drink Water, Feel Great!**
(1) ________ you feel tired in class? Drink water! (2) ________, bring a water bottle to school. (3) ________, drink some water at recess. (4) ________ will feel fresh and happy. You (5) ________ try it today!

## Quest 4: Title Hero (+10 XP each)

Make a fun title for each article.

1. An article about sleep: ______________________________
2. An article about playing football: ______________________________
3. An article about saving water: ______________________________

---

## Final Boss: Write Your Article (50 XP)

Write an article for the class blog: **Fun Things to Do in the School Holidays**. Write **50–80 words**.

**Ideas:** read books · play sports · help at home · your own idea!

**Helper frame** (you can use it):

> **Title:** ______________________  *by* ______________
> Do you ______________________? Read on!
> First, you can ______________________.
> Also, you can ______________________.
> Finally, you can ______________________.
> The holidays are ____________. Try it today!

**XP checklist**

- [ ] Fun title (10 XP)
- [ ] A question at the start (10 XP)
- [ ] 3 tips with First, Also, Finally (15 XP)
- [ ] I talk to “you” (5 XP)
- [ ] Advice at the end (10 XP)

---

## Sample answers (for discussion in class)

**Sample A: Good (about 50 words, uses the helper frame)**

> **Holiday Fun!**
> *by Hafiz, 1 Dahlia*
> Do you want a fun holiday? Read on! First, you can read books. It is easy. Also, you can play football with your friends. Finally, you can help your mother at home. The holidays are fun. Try it today!

**Sample B: Even better (about 70 words, adds details and wow words)**

> **Super Holiday Ideas!**
> *by Nurul Ain, 2 Cempaka*
> Are you bored in the school holidays? Don’t worry! First, you can read exciting books. You can visit the library near your house. Also, you can play sports like badminton or football. It is good for your body. Finally, you can help your family at home. You can cook or clean with them. Holidays are amazing. You should try these ideas today!

**Talk about it**

1. Find the TITLE, HELLO, TIPS and BYE parts in Sample A.
2. Circle every “you” in Sample B. How many can you find?
3. What wow words does Sample B use? (*exciting, amazing*)
4. What extra details does Sample B add after each tip?
5. How would a **report** about the holidays be different? (*It would say what happened, use the past tense — “I read books”, “I played football” — and not talk to “you”.*)
